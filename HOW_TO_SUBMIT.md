# How to Finish and Submit Today

## 1. Open the prototype
- Download and unzip `eyfi_reward_ladder_submission.zip`.
- Open `index.html` in Google Chrome.
- No installation or coding setup is required.

## 2. Check the design
- Drag the registrations slider.
- Click different milestone cards.
- Click **Play 7-sec demo** to automatically show all unlocks.

## 3. Record the required 1–10 second video
The easiest method on Windows:
1. Open `index.html` in Chrome and maximize the window.
2. Press `Win + Alt + R` to start Xbox Game Bar recording.
3. Click **Play 7-sec demo**.
4. Press `Win + Alt + R` again after the final milestone unlocks.
5. The recording is usually saved in `Videos/Captures`.

Alternative: use Loom, start recording only the browser tab, click the demo button, then stop around 8 seconds.

## 4. Attach these two required files
- Your short MP4 video.
- `submission_notes.md`.

## 5. Optional but recommended
Deploy the prototype and include the link in the email:
- Go to Netlify Drop.
- Drag the unzipped folder onto the page.
- Copy the generated public URL.

## 6. Suggested file names
- `Briony_Sayani_EYFI_Reward_Ladder.mp4`
- `Briony_Sayani_EYFI_Submission_Notes.md`

## 7. Final checks
- Keep the video under 10 seconds.
- Ensure the recording clearly shows at least three reward unlocks.
- Mention that you are comfortable with the full-time, in-person Koramangala internship.
- Submit before the stated July 29, 2026 end-of-day deadline.
