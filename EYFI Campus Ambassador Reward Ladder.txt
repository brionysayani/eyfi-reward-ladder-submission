EYFI Campus Ambassador Reward Ladder

- I designed and developed a responsive, interactive reward ladder that transforms the EYFI ambassador milestones into a gamified progression journey. 

- I developed the module as a lightweight, self-contained HTML prototype with embedded CSS and vanilla JavaScript, making it easy to preview, deploy and integrate without additional dependencies.

- The experience includes:

-> a registration slider, 
-> clickable milestones, 
-> locked/unlocked/current states, 
-> live next-reward calculations, 
-> milestone celebrations(confetti), 
-> a mobile-friendly vertical layout, and 
-> a seven-second automated demo;

- I also refined the design through multiple Git commits to align it with EYFI’s visual identity and the exact reward requirements.
